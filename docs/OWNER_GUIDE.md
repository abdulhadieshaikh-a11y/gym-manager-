# Gym Manager: owner's guide

This guide covers everything you need for daily work. You only need a web browser: Google Chrome is recommended, on a laptop or phone.

## Opening the system

- Open Chrome and go to your gym's address (your IT person will give it to you, e.g. `https://yourgym.vercel.app`).
- Tip: bookmark it, or in Chrome use **⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app** to get a desktop icon.

## Logging in and out

- Enter your **email** and **password**, then press **Log in**.
- To log out, click your name in the top-right corner and choose **Log out**. Always log out on a shared computer.
- **Forgot your password?** On the login page, click **Forgot password?**, enter your email and open the link you receive. If no email arrives, ask your IT person to reset it.
- **To change your password:** go to **Settings → Your account**.

## Keep your login private

Never share your password. Your account can see all members' details and money records, and it can void payments. Every important action is recorded under the account that did it. If a receptionist needs access, ask your IT person to create a separate **staff** login. Staff can add members, take payments and check people in, but they cannot void payments, change prices or edit settings.

## First-time setup (once)

1. **Settings:** enter the gym name, phone, address and a receipt note, and upload your logo. Press **Save settings**.
2. **Packages:** create your plans, for example *Monthly – 1 month – Rs 3,000*, *3 Months*, *6 Months* and *Annual*.

## Adding a member

1. Go to **Members → Add member**.
2. Enter the name and phone number. Email and notes are optional. Leave **Member ID** empty to have one generated (GM-0001, GM-0002 …).
3. Under **Membership**, choose a package. Check the start date and price, and change the price if you are giving a discount. The box shows the expiry date.
4. Press **Add member**. The member's page opens, showing the membership fee as **Unpaid**.

If the phone number already belongs to another member, you will see a warning. Press **Save anyway** if that's correct (for example, family members).

## Recording fees

On a member's page, under **Fees**:

- **Record payment:** use this when the member pays part or all of a fee. Enter the amount received, the date and the method (Cash, Bank transfer, Easypaisa, JazzCash, Other). For transfers, enter the transaction ID in **Transaction reference**.
- **Mark as paid:** the same as above, with the full remaining balance already filled in.
- If someone pays only part, the fee shows **Partially paid** with the remaining balance until the rest is paid.
- **Add fee:** use this for other charges such as an admission fee.

**Important:** for bank transfers, Easypaisa and JazzCash, check that the money has actually arrived **before** you record it. The system records what you enter. It does not check your bank.

### Printing a receipt

After recording a payment, click **View / print receipt** in the message that appears. You can also click **Receipt** next to any payment in the member's **Payment history**, or on **Fees & Payments → Payments received**. Then press **Print receipt**. To send it on WhatsApp, choose **Save as PDF** in the print window.

### Fixing a mistake

You can't delete payments, which keeps your records trustworthy. If a payment was entered wrongly:

1. Click **Void** next to the payment and type the reason (e.g. "Wrong amount").
2. The money goes back onto the member's balance, and the payment stays in the history marked **Void**.
3. Record the correct payment.

If a fee should not be charged at all (for example, it was waived), click **Void** next to an unpaid fee.

## Seeing who hasn't paid

- On the **Dashboard**, look at **Outstanding fees**, **Of which overdue** and the **Unpaid & overdue fees** list.
- **Fees & Payments → Member fees** shows unpaid and partially paid fees by default. Choose **Overdue** in the filter to see only the late ones.
- **Members** has a **Has unpaid fees** filter.

A fee is **Overdue** when its due date has passed and there is still a balance.

## Renewing a membership

On the member's page, press **Renew**. The start date is set to the day after the current membership ends, so no days are lost. Choose the package, confirm the price and press **Save membership**. A new fee is created. Older memberships stay in **Membership history**.

## Attendance

1. Go to **Attendance**.
2. Type part of the member's name, ID or phone number.
3. Press **Check in**.

- Each member can check in **once per day**.
- If a member's membership has expired, the system stops the check-in. You can renew them first, or press **Allow check-in** with a reason (for example, "Paying tomorrow"). The reason is saved.
- Use **Check out** to record when they leave (optional).
- To see another day, use **Previous day** or pick a date. To see one member's full history, open their profile and click **View all** under Attendance.

## Trainers

**Trainers → Add trainer**: enter the name, phone and specialization. Open a trainer to edit their details, or **Deactivate** them when they leave.

## Members who leave

On the member's page, under **Member status**, press **Deactivate member** and give a reason. Their history is kept. You can **Reactivate** them any time.

## Dashboard

The dashboard shows today's collections and check-ins, this month's collections, money still owed, active, expired and expiring members, and charts of monthly fee collection, member growth and daily attendance. "Today" and "this month" follow the gym's local time (Pakistan time by default). Click any figure to open the matching list.

## How your data is saved

Everything is saved immediately to a secure online database the moment you see the green confirmation message. Nothing is stored only on your computer. You can close the browser, switch devices or log in from your phone and see the same information. Ask your IT person to keep regular backups.

## Getting help

If a page shows an error, check your internet connection and press **Try again**. If it keeps happening, note what you were doing and contact your IT person.
